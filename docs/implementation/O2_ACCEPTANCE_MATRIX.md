## Levels, windows and eligibility

The stationary fixture **S** is three seconds of same-epoch M4/O1 at fs=48000, f=82.41/196/440 Hz, V=.10 m/s, R=1.2/1.8/2.4, k=RV/1.65. **H** uses 1318.51 Hz, R=1.8, V=.10 at 32/48/96/192 kHz. Start filters at zero. Unless overridden below, measure [2,3) s, uncentered RMS, one lane, σ=+1, G=E=C=t=1, d=0, neutral identity. S/H run each Noise depth 0/.5/1 with identical samples. R endpoints are prescribed pressure stresses. Movement does not enter O2. These controls isolate the transfer. Integrated S5 must also run the actual Movement and Saturation endpoints, source changes, holds and a factory-noise fixture. Character is full wet for wet comparisons. Out and protection are bypassed throughout the V17 comparison.

For eligible amplitude ratios use 20 log10(max(RMS(num)/RMS(den),1e−12)). The −240 dB floor is applied to the **ratio**, after division. Exact-zero residual with a voiced denominator is −240 dB. Zero or sub-floor denominator is unavailable. Preserve physical absolute amplitudes, mean, uncentered/AC RMS, peak, decay windows and durations even in silence. A voiced spectral window requires AC RMS >1e−12 in its own units (m/s for physical, FS for audio). Phase and relative partial level additionally require peak amplitude >1e−12 in those units for each involved harmonic, and at least two target periods for relative phase/envelopes. For complex transfer both windows and both partials must qualify. For summed-power ratios require a voiced denominator with positive non-DC power. At equality or exact silence use JSON null for unavailable phase, ratio or normalized statistic, never a fabricated phase, NaN or infinity. A first-sample window supplies only time-domain data. Short onset projections are window summaries. A stationary voiced interval is required for pitch acceptance. Lift tails, vibrato and onset cannot qualify a stationary gate. Keep the 1e−12 diagnostic floor distinct from V04/V12 voicing and partial-count thresholds.

## Nominal limiter use and integrated recovery

**Proposed exact nominal test for S6R.** Reset, 48 kHz, 64-frame partition plus final partial block. Prepend 4800 silent frames. Generate 144000 frames of the original 8-partial pluck at 196 Hz, τ=.28 s, 1 ms rise, θ[0]=0, phase .13k², increments 2π196/fs. Scale the entire *input fixture* once to .35 FS peak in f64, then cast to f32. The executable records the padded mono little-endian sample digest. Instrument input gain=1. Append 62400 zero frames. Test mono, identical stereo, opposite-polarity stereo and left-only stereo. Use Attack=20 ms, Character=1, Noise=0, Saturation=.5, Movement=0, public Out=.5. Repeat Noise=.5 with the same stimulus and qualification count. Use neutral identity. Grade every layout/depth separately over exactly the **144000 pluck frames**, including acquisition, hold and ordinary release within that interval. Report pre/post-roll separately so padding cannot dilute the fraction. Record active-voice/fully-wet frame counts and require confirmed launch, actual E>0 and zero nominal faults. A direct-only fallback cannot pass this test.

For a deterministic routing control, inject V=.10 m/s, R=1.8, G=1, σ=+ on the positive lane and − on its inverted twin. Start nominal M4 at first pluck frame with E=0, rise linearly to 1 over 960 samples and hold to pluck end. Lift on first post-roll frame with a 4800-frame linear E fade. Keep silent lane idle. This is a separate run. For the **live nominal gate**, use exactly the A3 gain G=clip(A_pk/.14,.08,1.4), signed peak bucket, V/R targets and 15 ms slews, linked direct Attack, and per-sample E from actual acquisition/hold/release. Log realized trajectories, peak buckets and capture times. Forcing G=1 or E=1 on this live test would change the nominal stimulus response. Log both runs and both noise settings. Stress runs separately force G=1.4/internal Out=2.

At each frame take p=max over channels of abs(f32 lane mix) times the slewed trim. Log instantaneous required gain r=min(1,.98/p) for p>0, otherwise 1. Instantaneous demand=max(0,20 log10(p/.98)) dB, zero at silence. With g initialized to 1, the shipping code uses g=min(r,1−(1−g_previous) exp(−1/(.050fs))). Record every channel pre/post, r, g and trim. Count one active frame per **linked frame** with applied g<1, including recovery, and independently count instantaneous requests p>.98. Use actual f64 g and no hidden meter deadband. Sub-unity roundoff during recovery may remain detectable even after inaudible attenuation. Preserve both counts. Require active/144000 <.01 (at most 1439 frames) and processed abs peak ≤.980001 FS over the entire stream. Verify the immediate downward response and sample-by-sample recovery against the recurrence, identical gain in both lanes, correct settled Out=0 silence, and re-entry after trim slew. No offline product or threshold crossing count qualifies this gate.

For S6R also run the full V08 ten-minute random/64-corner stereo overload/NaN suite, warm and cold trim automation with maximum step 2/(.010fs), mute/repick/release and deliberate one-lane corruption. ## Discriminating controls and remaining owners

The executable matrix rejects unwarped coefficients, wrong denominator sign, unstable response, fixed cutoff coefficients at the wrong single rate, omitted blocker, dirty reset, reordered gain at the a tap, duplicate .85 and trim, excess detail, nonzero silence, per-render normalization, a self-referenced RMS result, a doubled wet level and an octave-dominant carrier. Exact zero, 1e−22 tails and a voiced numerator with zero denominator exercise the actual O1 reporter. A forged tail phase must fail. The ratio floor is tested at zero and below floor. Per-rate errors and per-case failed predicates are retained, including identity stress violations. Unconditional checks plus the existing optimized-Python guard keep acceptance active. Constants and fixed vectors live in the two checked-in runners. Generated results, clips and traces live under ignored build outputs.

Retain V18's zero heap activity, no blocking, 60 s event/reset workload and 30 s denormal workload ≤2× warmed silence. V19 owns exact event sample times, reset repeatability, partition peak error ≤1e−6 FS, ABI failures and aliasing. V20 owns equivalent input at all seven rates, pitch, wet RMS spread <3 dB, timing within one hop plus two samples, fallback/tail and host guards. V21 requires real package, memory ≤.8 MiB, measured p50/p99/p99.9/max and overruns, ≥60 s per configuration, ≥10 min stress, <10/20% mean core at 48/192 kHz stereo B=64, p99.9 <.5 deadline for B≥16, zero DSP-caused misses. V22 requires all three original integrated listening comparisons and documented failures. Original detailed requirements and staged owners continue to apply.
