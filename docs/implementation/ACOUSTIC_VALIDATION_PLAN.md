### Historical Stage 03 and 04 replay

Run this historical replay from the repository root with the pinned Rust toolchain on PATH and Python with NumPy/SciPy. The saved reference WAVs and hashes below allow an offline replay. No network, ignored Review 3 script, host or pitch selector is required. The qualification writes approximately 4.5 GB to the ignored build directory.

```sh
export PATH="$HOME/.cargo/bin:$PATH"
export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 VECLIB_MAXIMUM_THREADS=1
py=build/spec-reconciliation/py/bin/python  # or a local Python with NumPy/SciPy
"$py" tools/validation/guide_qualification.py
"$py" tools/validation/guide_negative_controls.py
```

## Gates at the earliest observable boundary

| Owner / test | Procedure and criterion for passing |
|---|---|
| Mathematical, spatial observation | Eager absolute-time N array including original M4 negative times. At every supported rate test s=1 cancellation, s=β junction equality and the three real stations, integer taps, fractional taps below 1, wrap, P lengthening across the seed seam and 4 oct/s retune. For the endpoint oracle only, use current N explicitly and the ideal nut subtraction. Compare Rust reads and weighted total before gain to eager values within 1e−9 m/s (V03 trajectory tolerance). Bit-compare a,b,B,N,j, branch and raw M5 against the unobserved guide. Sign swap, single ray, one-sample shift and accidental second reflection must fail. |
| Mathematical, electrical/identity transfer | Compare f64 Rust impulse spectra with the exact complex O2 rational function (including DC and gain), and squared positive/negative complex poles in I1. Use input and output impulses of sufficient duration that the analytic remaining tail is <1e−10 of peak. Require ≤1e−9 absolute complex error for normalized unit inputs, comparable to V03 differential precision. Log phase/group delay in passbands, record zeros separately. Reject stable-but-wrong swapped sign, conjugate image omission, old z1 for z2, wrong taper or accidental resonator feedback. Zero vector returns q exactly, zero signal and reset give all zeros. |
| Isolated, analyzer feature | Compare direct Hann sums against scheduled/rotating implementation for every K at 65,196,440,1320 Hz and rate endpoints. Use matched phases, ±polarity, two gains above floor, DC, one partial, silent frame, near-floor noise, deep/weak/supported dips and beyond-band partials. Compare gains to oracle within 1e−8 dB away from threshold ties, verify declared equality rules at ties; g stays within 10^(±5/20). Compare output on opposite polarity inputs and fresh seeds. Future input changes must leave earlier outputs identical. |
| Isolated, dynamics and stability | V03–V11/V20 raw gates and transactional faults remain mandatory. Add O1/O2, then source transfer with identical controls. V06 pressure raises upper energy on the qualified guide; V07 speed ratio holds. Quantify final harmonic envelopes at each endpoint and check the sign/direction of observed contrasts. Measure peak, finite state, impulse decay, post-mute tails, instantaneous weighting bounds at all 64 knob corners; don't infer gain bounds from ≤5 dB per band. |
| Baseline injected S4a (authorized) | Authoritative A3 launch at E=0 and captured V/R, qualified M4 and per-lane M6 cache. Verify acquisition, hold, lift, fault and replacement on actual epochs with injected frames and M5 or O1. |
| Proposed stroke amendment (held) | Launch at E=0, seed V=.06 for proposed stroke. Confirm no hard entry step in Ew. Log actual V,R,k, g, E, epoch and wet for soft/hard accents, same-note repicks, legato replacements, slow fades, strong mute and unsupported loud input. Compare every sample of prescribed trajectory and fades to A3/M6, including fault sample and simultaneous stereo activity. Running the new observer must give the same guide trace as rendering those V/R commands alone. V10/V11 pitch windows stay fixed; lifecycle sampled on every tick, also through 60 s hold. |
| Combined S4b/S6R, live identity | For the three paired G3 passages and independent same-string pickups: pitch/level matched, fixed virtual bow first, then real A3 controls logged identically for comparison. Use held full-wet segment after direct coefficient reaches zero and source support ends; before hold expiration, compare normalized H1–H12 distances, four broad log-band means, signed notch depth and tilt. Report dropped/invalid frames and unsupported partials; omit no failed fixture. During fast bends enforce the 25-cent veto, preserve the last gain target; compatible reacquisition smooths without a reset. Weak silence cannot re-open or refresh a hold. Blank/corrupt/mismatched-epoch jobs cannot apply. |
| Combined, identity decision | Predeclare which dip or broad cue is repeatable on the **direct** calibration takes. If the direct repeats themselves overlap between sources, that pair is uninformative and the decision stays open. Freeze a numerical margin from the maximum same-condition repeat distance including capture-window sensitivity and measurement repeat error. Use held-out takes: between-source held distance must exceed that margin, matched cue must keep its sign and the listener must match both references. Use the same metric and units on all sides. Set/approve thresholds before revealing outputs. Report between-string/player results separately. A nonzero distance or static transfer fit alone does not pass this gate. |
| S5, V17/V20 | Compute common-gain single-ray, spatial-only, electrically loaded and final wet outputs on the old stationary and high-note grids. Also report the fixed nominal trim. Apply V17's ±3 dB coloration, strongest-fundamental, η≥.85 and factory noise residual ≤−28 dB rules at the defined measurement points, and grade final wet against original converted M5 as the authoritative end-to-end comparison. Sweep 65–4000 Hz on the electrical/transfer branch at frozen f, flag narrow >6 dB peaks; spatial nulls have analytical frequencies, not automatic failure as filter instability. Run Noise 0/.5/1, Movement endpoints on changing source frames, Saturation endpoints via pressure, neutral identity and worst credible held vectors. Failure to meet original meaningful limits requires review. V20 final wet spread <3 dB at equivalent inputs. |
| S5, alias/noise/gesture | Compare 32/48/96 kHz high note with independent ≥96 kHz render low-passed/decimated (document anti-alias filter). Log harmonic powers, residual masks, ≥6 kHz energy and pitch-dependent sidebands. Subtract same-source Noise=0/Noise=.5 renders for V17; lift zeros contact work. Log first sample, AC/uncentered RMS 0–10/50 ms, steady RMS, 25 ms harmonic slices in entry/exit and phase-aligned cycle residual. Fixed-speed constant-amplitude excitation plus matched E is the negative gesture control. An improved attack needs a listener decision. |
| S6R/H/P | Retain V08 linked .98 protection and limiter-use limits, V13–V16 silence, mute/hysteresis, fallback/anti-phase, V18 heap/locks/denormals, V19 partition/ABI/bypass, V20 rate/tail. ## Compact human architecture gate

The [listening pack](../../build/stage-04/listening-comparisons.zip) contains the six anonymous examples, three references, questions and recording credits. Allow about a minute for the three comparisons. Listen at a fixed playback level, record route, date, listener and any timestamped fault. Do not reveal the generated `listening-key.json` until answers are recorded. “Indistinguishable” and “neither” are valid outcomes. No parameter tuning. The gate can be replayed by a reviewer independent of development.

| Comparison | Listen | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Bow character | Hear the [physical reference](../../tools/validation/fixtures/acoustic/vaisvil-8.wav), then [Timbre A](../../build/stage-04/timbre-A.wav) and [Timbre B](../../build/stage-04/timbre-B.wav). The physical excerpt is low E; the comparison is 196 Hz on both sides. | Which sounds more like a bowed electric guitar? Which buzzes? Flag any objectionable hiss, whistle or resonance. |
| Held identity | Hear [DI 1](../../build/stage-04/identity-reference-1.wav) and [DI 2](../../build/stage-04/identity-reference-2.wav), then [held A](../../build/stage-04/identity-A.wav) and [held B](../../build/stage-04/identity-B.wav). All nominal G3; identical bow controls. | Match each held sound to a DI and name the cue. If neither has recognizable guitar identity, record that. |
| Stroke | [Attack A](../../build/stage-04/attack-A.wav) and [Attack B](../../build/stage-04/attack-B.wav) have the same E schedule, selected output transfer, lift and terminal bow settings. Their realized early amplitudes can differ. | Which entrance sounds more naturally bowed? Did the lift sound like a release? Flag discontinuity, excessive buzz or resonance. |

Engineering comparisons preserve gains. Listening copies target .10 FS RMS using [.2,1) s for timbre, the late held interval for identity and [.25,.5) for stroke, with one common attenuation per pair if a peak would exceed .90. Timbre/held listening cuts have 5 ms endpoint fades; matched RMS differs by <.5 dB. The attack window retains its actual envelope. Raw direct references are converted to 48 kHz for audition only and separately level-matched. The randomized order is repeatable; a second listener or rerun may use a new hidden assignment, recorded with the trial. Keep original-gain files for overload judgments.

Advance the direction only if the selected output is preferred for bow/buzz, both identity matches and the named cue are correct, and the selected attack wins with a usable release and no objectionable noise/resonance. A tie, missing evidence or stated fault leaves the corresponding gate open. One listener and one binary pairing cannot establish statistical recognition. Confirm on the untouched pickup repeats before committing live integration. Record the actual human answer verbatim and compare to the objective repeatability gate; never infer a listening verdict from these measurements.
