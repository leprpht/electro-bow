**2026-11-23 · Production baseline verified.** ElectroBow 1.6.0 at `20d4ee56e8f614e6544bf3456698fa5d3c096887` runs the six-control timbral instrument. Its source, tests, manifests and host adapter are byte-identical to audit pin `aa1be375020f81b223171cc8feed52b50dccb7d2`. The intervening diff adds the seven design documents and the energy figure. A direct-sum A2 experiment finds a consistent octave error between the named V12 pitches. Two gate-wording conflicts, a migration dependency and a pre-existing extreme-rate tail overflow also need dispositions. The current binary and reference measurements remain usable independently of those decisions. [Commands, binary identity and results](IMPLEMENTATION_LEDGER.md)

Line numbers refer to this checkout. **Verified** means source inspection, algebra or a named baseline measurement establishes the stated claim. For a target equation this establishes its meaning or feasibility only. **Uncertain** identifies missing fixtures, definitions or implementation evidence. **Conflicting** identifies requirements that give different answers for the same case. Proposed resolutions do not amend the seven source documents.
