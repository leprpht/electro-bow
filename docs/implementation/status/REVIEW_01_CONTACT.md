## Contact law and branch selection

Continuity gives `v=B+b=N+a`. Both outgoing waves therefore gain the same velocity `j=v−(a+b)`, giving `B=a+j` and `N=b+j`. Each arm requires force `Zj`, so `F=2Zj`. For bow velocity `V`, signed slip `r=V−v` and incident mismatch `d=(V−a)−b`, the signed load line is `r+j=d`.

- Lift gives `j=0, r=d`, free scattering and cleared mode memory. With positive normal load, static contact gives `r=0, j=d` in the static interval `|j|≤kμ₀`, where `k=F_N/(2Z)`. The sliding equality is inapplicable to static contact.
- On slide, `j=sign(r)·kμ(|r|)`. Since `μ>0`, slip and mismatch have the same sign. In positive coordinates, `h=|d|=q+kμ(q)` with `q=|r|>0`. The admissible slide is the largest intersection on an increasing segment, a coincident interval or the constant-μ tail. Previous Stick or an opposite-sign slide gives static priority when admissible. A same-sign slide keeps the admissible upper root, including at a fold. Above the static limit, reversal can slide immediately. Zero mismatch sticks. The separately specified slip floor applies after root selection.
- The quadratic difference of outgoing and incident velocity flux expands to `Z[(a+j)²+(b+j)²−a²−b²]=2Zj(a+b+j)=Fv`. Bow power minus string power is `FV−Fv=Fr`, nonnegative for the exact friction law. V02 supplies a numerical dissipation allowance of `−1e−12 W`. At `Z=1`, the separate checks are signed mismatch and branch-specific residuals in m/s, force bound in N and flux/dissipation in W. Rust forms `d` before branch selection and rejects negative or nonfinite load, nonfinite mismatch and overflow of the static limit. A nonfinite incident input cannot give a finite `d`. Prepared coefficients are positive and bounded above by `μ₀`, so a finite static product bounds every other product. Near the largest finite value, an addend no larger than 1.28 is too small to overflow a rounded load. In the selected segment the ordered enclosure ensures finite nonnegative differences and a positive divisor except in the endpoint path, which takes no division. The tail difference is finite. This bounds slip and keeps `j=d−r` finite. The solver checks `F, v, B, N` before constructing its result. Its immutable receiver, value arguments and payload-free error leave mode, ring and FIR storage with the caller. ### MINOR · Exact-oracle mode comparison can reject the specified slip-floor decision

**Location and requirement.** The offline oracle rounds the exact root once and applies the floor to that value. V02 then compares modes exactly. M3b applies the floor to the result of the specified fraction-first binary64 operations, which may differ by an allowed ULP. [Oracle interpolation, floor and comparison](../../../tools/validation/contact_contract.py#L15) · [Comparison assertion](../../../tools/validation/contact_contract.py#L117) · [Rust floor](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/friction.rs#L89) · [V02 comparison text](../DSP_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#L109)

**Counterexample.** Replay `1.7431847738615466e-12 4.5041501448100105e-13 1` and its signed mirror with previous `-1`. Rust computes the selected magnitude as `1e−12` and returns Stick with `j=d`. The independent rational root rounds to `1.0000000000000002e−12`, so `graph(d,k,1)` returns Slide(+) with `j=7.431847738615464e−13` m/s. The signed mirror gives the corresponding false mismatch. This is far below the normalized playing load. The mandatory grid and fold cases pass.

**Consequence and required remediation for generalized V02 replay.** Preserve independent root enumeration and ULP grading. When the independent selected root is within its interpolation-error band of the floor, use the chosen segment or tail and the normative operation order to adjudicate the exact floor decision. Retain exact mode grading elsewhere and add both signed counterexamples plus neighboring queries. Rerun the full Python V02 differential/negative-control suite and Rust slip-floor regression when changing the validator. The current Rust behavior on these vectors follows M3b.
