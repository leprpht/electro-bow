## C. Mathematical qualification and findings

### Observation and loading

With x increasing toward the nut, write u as right- and left-going displacement. Then v=v₊+v₋ and `T u_x=Z(v₋−v₊)` for `Z=T/c_s`. At the bow that means B−b on the bridge side and a−N on the nut side, each multiplied by Z to obtain the tension slope contribution in N. At a rigid bridge the signed force exerted by the string on the support is `Z(v_in−v_out)`, with magnitude `2Z|v_in|`, while bridge velocity and transmitted power are zero. For a dynamic bridge, `v_b/F_b=Y` in m/(N·s), `R=(ZY−1)/(ZY+1)` and `F_b v_b` is instantaneous mechanical power. At the junction M6 separately yields `2Zj(a+b+j)=Fv`. Radiativity is receiver-dependent Pa/N. Displacement from velocity at nonzero ω divides by iω and requires an independent DC reference in time integration. The chosen velocity sensor directly evaluates `−∫κ(x)v(x)dx`, where κ is the local flux-linkage derivative per unit spatial length, yielding V before arbitrary audio scaling. This path requires neither a displacement integrator nor a fictitious allocation of the bridge's lumped FIR.

O1 uses the two retarded times `d₁=(s−β)P/2`, `d₂=(2−s−β)P/2`. At s=β the sum is N+a, and at s=1 it cancels exactly. The oldest station delay is .8555P, fitting the existing capacity even with the post-commit offset and interpolation neighbor. The minimum short delay is .42424 samples at 32 kHz/1320 Hz. Before write, n is the cursor. After commit it is n+1, so the integer lag is `floor(d)+1` with the original fractional part. At d<1 the committed N[n] is essential. Negative-time samples use the retained launch P/V even when the current P changes. Six convex reads followed by positive weights bound |vₚ| by 8 m/s. Invalid guide ticks have no observed sample. The approximation evaluates both delays at present P during retune, consistent with the guide's time-varying coordinates. It gives no physical string/fret estimate or distributed-loss reconstruction. [O1](../../architecture/ELECTROBOW_ACOUSTIC_OUTPUT_SPECIFICATION.md#o1-reconstruct-the-sensed-quantity) · [Tick ordering](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/waveguide.rs#L151) · [Ring indexing](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/ring.rs#L72)

An ideal rectangular aperture multiplies the sinusoidal modal term by sinc(kπw/2). With these three weighted stations Simpson gives `(2+cos(kπw/2))/3` in the ideal harmonic model. The continuous aperture has its first null near order 83.3, while the approximation never zeroes its aperture multiplier. The positional first null is near order 5.75. Nominal loading makes the high-order discrepancy less audible on these runs. The discrete tap interpolator further modifies magnitude and phase. The approved scope is the specified three-point **observation** and a numerical oracle, with geometry treated as one effective reference setting.

O2's DC blocker is `c(1−z⁻¹)/(1−cz⁻¹)`. Its pole is .996081… .999346 over the rate limits, and its zero is DC. Bilinear prewarp maps the analog Butterworth pair in the left half-plane inside the unit disk. The low-pass has unit DC gain, a double Nyquist zero, −3.0103 dB at 3.2 kHz and no resonant peak. At 48 kHz the numerator is (.03357181,.06714362,.03357181), denominator (1,−1.41898265,.55326989). Across seven tested rates pole radii were .642496… .928627. The blocker and DFII-T implementation are causal, f64, and zero initialized on launch. At Noise>0 the detail branch fills the exact load's Nyquist zero with a small parallel path. Phase and group delay are frequency-dependent and can change rapidly near spatial nulls. The measured 440 Hz H1–H7 delay relative to outgoing N emission was 1.069–1.104 ms. Frozen ideal spatial delay is `(1−β)P/2`, approximately 0.992 ms at 440 Hz and 6.72 ms at 65 Hz. The causal time-varying observer has no single fixed group delay. Publish both .85 engineering gain and .85/√2 nominal gain, and apply captured sign and G once after the filters.

### I1 and end-to-end contract

The proposed analysis is original sanitized pre-swell DI → shared 30 Hz HP → a separate unity-DC Blackman sinc and integer decimator → four-period mean-removed Hann projections. On 5 ms hops, it subtracts a reliability-weighted mean of log(k·peak amplitude), with floor max(1e−5,3N₀,.015 max A). Reliability ramps from zero at ε to one at 4ε, and two supported orders are required. `.45rδ` is clipped at ±5 dB. Tiny or absent partials cannot trigger inverse gain. Unreliable whole frames freeze the vector. Per-order partial dropouts target unity for that order on an otherwise valid frame. The order representation follows retuning via pole rotations with frequency taper, no phase replacement or periodic output independent of the guide. Squaring the single-pole response follows from the updated z1 in z2. The real carrier requires conjugate images. Time-varying rotations with unit phase and ρ<1 give a contraction for both states and a loose conservative output bound `G(1+24·.779)sup|q|`. That bound does not establish acceptable gain, frequency balance or no excitation of transient filter ringing. A true sensor null cannot be recovered by boosting its missing carrier component.
