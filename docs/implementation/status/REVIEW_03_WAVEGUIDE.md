### Mathematical and code review

- **M2 propagation and absolute timing.** Unfolding the round trips gives bridge distance $2\beta L$ and nut distance $2(1-\beta)L$. Each termination enforces zero transverse velocity, so its velocity reflection has sign −1. With $P=f_s/f$, the nut delay is $(1-\beta)P$. The bridge FIR is $-.98e^{-i\omega}(.75+.25\cos\omega)$, whose real bracket stays positive and contributes one sample of phase delay. The bridge interpolator delay is therefore $\beta P-1$. With $D=m+u$, the two-tap transfer is $e^{-i\omega m}(1-u+u e^{-i\omega})$. Its magnitude is at most one and its phase delay is $m+\operatorname{atan2}(u\sin\omega,1-u+u\cos\omega)/\omega$. The signed reflections cancel in the circuit, and $D_B+D_N+1=P$ apart from fractional phase error. At $\omega=2\pi\cdot1320/32000$, extremizing the fractional residual in $u$ gives about .001081 samples per arm. In Rust, the nut history produces $a$, the bridge FIR produces $b$, and the junction sends $a+j$ toward the bridge and $b+j$ toward the nut. [M2](../../architecture/BOWED_STRING_MATHEMATICS.md#L15) · [Tick routing](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/waveguide.rs#L151) · [Interpolation](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/ring.rs#L81)
- **M3 scattering and energy.** Continuity on both sides gives $v=B+b=N+a=a+b+j$. The two outgoing waves increase by $j=F/(2Z)$. Rust evaluates $d=(V-a)-b$ in that order. Subtracting $v$ from $V$ gives $r+j=d$. The reviewed solver uses the stored M3b load graph, retains the same-sign upper sliding branch when admissible, uses static contact under the specified prior-mode rules, and propagates with $j=0$ and Stick memory after lift. The guide passes and then commits the actual mode. At $Z=1$, the measured net flux $B^2+N^2-a^2-b^2$ agreed with $Fv$ to <1e−10 W, and $Fr\ge-10^{-12}$ W across all 152 traces. Final driven junction work averaged +.002264, +.002239 and +.002678 W at 65.41, 82.41 and 440 Hz respectively. Lift gives exactly zero contact power. This matches the energy source implied by the recurrence. The only energy before the first tick is the finite prescribed launch history. [Solver and force](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/friction.rs#L64) · [Branch commit](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/waveguide.rs#L207) · [M6 power balance](../../architecture/BOWED_STRING_MATHEMATICS.md#L109)
- **M4 launch and retune.** Let $S(t)=t-\lfloor t\rfloor-.5$, $A=V/\beta$. Negative-time $B=A S(n/P_0)$, $N=-A S(n/P_0+1-\beta)$ each give $V$ for the stick interval and $V-A$ on slip when combined with their ideal reflected wave. Preload $x[-1],x[-2]$ from the bridge prefix at the same tap delay with both integer lags shifted appropriately. Launch opens both epochs, installs the preload and sets Slide(+). For lag $l\le n$ the ring reads a committed wave. Otherwise it evaluates $n-l$ at the original launch $P_0,A$. Once the count is saturated at capacity all legal reads are stored history. The tick uses current $f$ for all tap delays, leaving launch $P_0,A$, prior loss and branch to their normal lifecycle. Linear interpolation has the same limiting value on either side of an integer delay. The maximum effective shifted bridge tap is $.127P+1$, the nut is $.873P$, and the pickup is $.0635P$. Their oldest integer neighbors fit in $C=\lceil f_s/65\rceil+4$. The caller owns slew, launch timing and gain. [M4](../../architecture/BOWED_STRING_MATHEMATICS.md#L86) · [Launch and preload](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/waveguide.rs#L81) · [History lookup](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/ring.rs#L99)
- **M5 pickup and M6 transaction.** Pickup is a pre-write read of outgoing B at $.0635P$, a single-wave bridge-force proxy. Output is $0.85c(y_{n-1}+p_n-p_{n-1})$ in FS at unit captured gain, with $c=\exp(-2\pi\cdot20/f_s)$ and $y$ expressed in m/s. Pickup and DC state stay outside feedback. The guide rejects invalid controls before division, validates previous states and unmodified candidate values, and stages each outgoing value through the inclusive ±4 m/s guard. Both stage tokens must exist before either commit. Dropping a token changes no slot or cursor. The two commits and scalar assignments are infallible under the established index bounds, and the guide's exclusive borrow makes the successful update indivisible to a caller. A failed transaction returns Fault on that tick and invalidates both epochs and all scalar feedback/DC state. It returns no audible proposal. [M5–M7](../../architecture/BOWED_STRING_MATHEMATICS.md#L101) · [Tick boundary](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/waveguide.rs#L120) · [Pre-commit guards](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/waveguide.rs#L186) · [Ring stage and commit](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/ring.rs#L116)

**Reachable Rust sample path.** The offline adapter reads binary64 `(Hz,V,k)`, calls `Guide::prepare` once, `launch → invalidate → ring.invalidate ×2 → seed → ring.launch ×2 → bridge.read_at ×2`, then `tick → transaction`. A live transaction calls `bridge.read → read_at → history` for loss, `nut.read → read_at → history` for $a$, computes negative FIR return $b$, performs a second bridge read for pickup, calls `FrictionCurve::solve → upper_slip` when needed, calculates DC and runs the proposal guards. It then calls `DelayRing::stage` twice, `PendingWrite::commit` twice and stores loss/mode/pickup/DC. The adapter serializes only returned telemetry and samples. The cleared branch exits before tap or contact calls. A failed transaction follows `tick → fault → invalidate → ring.invalidate ×2`. The only seed reads after launch resolve missing negative-time lags. An expired-seed test drained the real circuit, reclosed the cut and alternated extreme delays. Both waves, both incidents, pickup and loss stayed exactly zero. There is no oscillator, ongoing external audio, noise generator, feedback gain compensation, limiter, automatic level adjustment or hidden seed source on this path. The stress scheduler's PRNG selects control points outside the guide. ### MAJOR · The existing pickup is a force proxy with an unspecified audible transfer

A string with tension $T$, linear density $\mu$, wave speed $c_s=\sqrt{T/\mu}$ and impedance $Z=T/c_s$ carries two transverse velocity waves. At a station $x$ increasing from the bridge toward the nut, $v=v_{\mathrm{in}}+v_{\mathrm{out}}$, $u(\omega)=v(\omega)/(i\omega)$ for nonzero frequency, and $T\partial_xu=Z(v_{\mathrm{in}}-v_{\mathrm{out}})$. The rigid limit reflects with $v_{\mathrm{out}}=-v_{\mathrm{in}}$. Bridge velocity is zero and reaction force has magnitude $2Z|v_{\mathrm{in}}|$. The near-bridge ramp is therefore a meaningful excitation proxy. Its arbitrary observation delay and gain do not describe acoustic radiation or magnetic sensing. The current FIR lumps losses around the bridge trip. It provides no unique physical split of those losses into propagation and moving termination. [M2/M5](../../architecture/BOWED_STRING_MATHEMATICS.md#L101) · [Woodhouse §5.1.2, equations 1–5](https://euphonics.org/5-1-1-coupling-a-string-to-the-instrument-body/)

| Quantity | What the existing state supports |
|---|---|
| Individual traveling velocity | Existing p, B, N and the two junction returns. A nominal bridge-arrival tap at delay βP/2 is already used as p. Its interpretation is approximate during retune. |
| Total velocity, slope, transverse displacement | Junction velocity is already reported by contact. Junction slope on either side follows from the two local waves and the corresponding direction signs. An interior station needs matched forward and return taps and a declared split for FIR timing/loss. Displacement additionally needs a reference or bounded band-limited integration with defined DC state. |
| Reaction force, bridge velocity, power | Approximate rigid force is $2Zp$ with sign selected by orientation. Force at a modified boundary can be evaluated as $Z(v_{\mathrm{in}}-v_{\mathrm{out}})$. To model motion use $v_b=Y_bF_b$ and reflection $(ZY_b-1)/(ZY_b+1)$ consistently. Boundary power is $F_bv_b$ instantaneously or $\tfrac12\Re(F_bv_b^*)$ per sinusoid using peak phasors. An exactly rigid bridge transfers zero mechanical power. Loop attenuation in M2 gives loss without identifying where the dissipated power goes. |
| Radiated pressure | Requires a calibrated force-to-pressure transfer $H_{pF}(\omega)$ with observation direction and distance. It is a different physical quantity from the driving-point mechanical admittance $Y_b$. An output-only approximation driven by rigid force can leave S2b feedback intact. |
| Magnetic pickup voltage | Faraday induction gives $e=-d\Phi/dt$. Linearizing flux around the rest position gives a weighted spatial integral of transverse velocity mapped through pickup and loading electronics. Aperture and position can impose harmonic nulls and the electrical load can add resonance. A chosen geometry needs both local waves, directional sensitivity and an output transfer. The current single-wave tap lacks those quantities. |

**Consequence and owner.** A two-wave velocity or displacement observation may yield a different waveform and can be physically appropriate for a specified sensor. Xorshift32 creates a shared signal filtered by the difference of 4,200 and 700 Hz one-poles. Scatter equals .80·Noise times that difference. Legacy Character uses $m=.66c(2-c)$ and trim $1+.90m$ on the attacked direct/body mix. M5 instead proposes an isolated split of the converted guide, .08 of present scatter depth, and $w=.85u+.15C(u)$ before A4. These are two distinct gain conventions. The proposed $w$ retains the large direct contribution from the **guide** even at full Character. At $cE=1$, A4 removes the shaped **guitar** contribution. [Body processing](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/body.rs#L69) · [Shipping mix](../../../rust/dsp/src/instrument/mod.rs#L90) · [M5](../../architecture/BOWED_STRING_MATHEMATICS.md#L101) · [A4](../../architecture/ELECTROBOW_TARGET_ARCHITECTURE.md#L73)

At 48 kHz, for Noise=Movement=Saturation=0, I evaluated the exact complex transfer of the inspected biquads and crossover, with fixed .45 pair blend. The response $|.85+.15C(e^{i\omega})|$ lies between −1.76 and −.15 dB at 1 Hz resolution from 65 Hz to Nyquist. The first seven harmonics at 440 Hz get −1.29, −1.57, −1.29, −.76, −1.65, −1.54 and −.91 dB. Relative to the fundamental this reshapes each by less than .54 dB. The result explains why the nominal parallel blend is unlikely to address the core buzz on its own. It also shows that the proposed coloration can satisfy benign level constraints while barely changing source character. The calculation covers the linear operating point. Nonzero controls change the transfer and can add intermodulation, sidebands or noise. No measurement here establishes that the nonlinear endpoints add convincing body or retain guitar identity. [Transfer calculation and output](../../../build/review-03/acoustic.py) · [Results](../../../build/review-03/acoustic.log) · [V17](../DSP_VALIDATION_PLAN.md#L78)

**Consequence and owner.** Preserve the shipping fallback and its regression fixtures. The existing splitter is a reusable causal utility, and the inexpensive modal recurrence may be reusable if its poles, zeros and input/output quantity follow from an agreed reference. The current frequencies, moving pair mix, saturation, scatter and .85/.15 ratio have empirical coloration roles and lack a calibrated connection to a bowed-guitar observation. More aggressive settings could disguise the source while increasing artificial resonances or residual noise. A small causal transfer is compatible in principle with the project's zero fixed transport-delay design. Its group delay, CPU and stereo costs need measurement with the selected response under V17–V21. Hold S5's new wet design and exit until the observation and transfer have research sign-off. Existing-path parity and crossover-only tests can continue under the prior authorization. ### MAJOR · The proposed full-wet output discards recoverable guitar timbre

Pitch contour, amplitude, polarity, onset/mute timing and support state retain performance identity. Original pick articulation and recorded pickup/amp tone remain on the direct path during acquisition and when $cE<1$. A3's brightness scalar can influence R but cannot encode a multi-harmonic envelope, pickup notches, multiple contributing coils or amp equalization. With fixed host controls and equivalent Hz, V/R, G and polarity histories, the deterministic wet path produces the same output for two inputs with different harmonic envelopes. In a held full-wet note the direct waveform coefficient is zero. A broadband spectral difference or a pickup null can therefore disappear even when the analyzer tracks both performances correctly. Captured gain is an overall scale, polarity is a sign and contact pressure changes the virtual string. Each can convey a cue without reproducing the original spectral balance. [A3 capture and hold](../../architecture/ELECTROBOW_TARGET_ARCHITECTURE.md#L54) · [A4 mix](../../architecture/ELECTROBOW_TARGET_ARCHITECTURE.md#L73) · [M7](../../architecture/BOWED_STRING_MATHEMATICS.md#L123)

**Consequence and owner.** Measure full-wet discrimination using the same pitches, playing gesture and level on different pickups, strings and instruments, including same-pitch notes on different strings. Determine which normalized partial or band relationships, spectral tilt and pickup-dependent notches remain stable after the pick transient and can be estimated causally from supported guitar input. Report conditioning during bends, weak partials, amplitude decay and loss of confidence, plus a finite held/fallback behavior. A bounded source-dependent transfer is a research requirement because the present architecture reduces substantially different inputs to the same control signal. It needs evidence that it preserves useful source cues without imposing pluck-only artifacts or removing bow-dependent variation. A one-microphone acoustic pressure target and a magnetic guitar pickup target have different identity cues. Choose the reference and units before selecting the transfer. [Pickup position, width and loading model](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=16551) · [Smith, spectral-envelope transfer](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/sasp/Applications_STFT.html)
