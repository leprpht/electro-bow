## Mathematics and actual Rust order

With $r=z^{-1}$, the blocker recurrence gives $H/V_p=c(1-r)/(1-cr)$ and $c=\exp(-2\pi\,20/f_s)$. Its zero is at $z=1$, its pole is $c\in(0,1)$, and its Nyquist gain is $2c/(1+c)$. A zero-history impulse starts at $c$ and then has weights $c^{n}(c-1)$ for $n\ge1$. Their absolute sum including the first weight is $2c$, giving a conservative blocker bound of $16c$ m/s for a legal input of magnitude at most 8 m/s.

For the load, begin with the unit-DC analog prototype $1/(1+\sqrt2\,u+u^2)$ and analog poles $u=(-1\pm i)/\sqrt2$. The bilinear map sends $u$ to $z=(1+Ku)/(1-Ku)$, with $K=\tan(\pi\,3200/f_s)$. This is prewarp equivalent to using analog cutoff $2f_sK$ rad/s. On the unit circle the normalized analog argument is $u=i\tan(\pi f/f_s)/K$, so the load magnitude at 3200 Hz is $1/\sqrt2$ and its phase is −90°. Multiplying the denominator by $K^2(1+r)^2$ gives the coefficient equations below. The open left half-plane maps inside the unit circle. Rust checks the equivalent real Jury inequalities $\lvert a_2\rvert<1$ and $\lvert a_1\rvert<1+a_2$ in addition to finiteness and the blocker pole.

```text
D = (1 + sqrt(2)*K) + K*K
b0 = K*K/D          b1 = 2*b0                     b2 = b0
a1 = 2*(K*K-1)/D    a2 = ((1-sqrt(2)*K)+K*K)/D

h  = c*((old_h + v) - old_v)
a  = (0.85*h)/sqrt(2)
l  = b0*a + old_z1
z1 = (b1*a - a1*l) + old_z2
z2 = b2*a - a2*l
q  = l + (0.025*depth)*(a-l)
```

Eliminating the two old-state values gives $(1+a_1r+a_2r^2)L=(b_0+b_1r+b_2r^2)A$. The recurrence is transposed direct form II with separately rounded operations in the listed order. The numerator is $b_0(1+r)^2$, and $4b_0=1+a_1+a_2$ in exact arithmetic. Thus the load has unity DC and a double Nyquist zero. The blocker still rejects cascade DC, and the parallel detail branch gives normalized Nyquist gain $.025\,d\,2c/(1+c)$ at depth $d$. Rust uses old `z2` for the first update, stores all four histories together after checking six candidate values, and returns `q`. Rate preparation contains the only exp/tan calls.

`v` and `h` and their retained histories are in m/s. The conversion is .85 FS/(m/s) followed once by dimensionless $1/\sqrt2$ before the load. `a`, `l`, `q`, `z1` and `z2` are in FS. The common engineering tap $U=\sqrt2q$ undoes the delivery trim for measurement only. The blend is a convex combination of the same blocked carrier and loaded carrier, with weights $(1-.025d,.025d)$. I found no second conversion or trim, per-note normalization, adaptive makeup, added source or coloration in the sample path. These constants specify a causal surrogate. Review 6's loaded-pickup example had a resonance while this neutral load is monotonic, and the available low-E fit constrains low-order geometry. A controlled target transfer with declared loading and calibrated velocity/voltage/recording gain is still needed for instrument-specific claims. ## Same-tick ownership, faults and partitioning

The actual observed route is `Harness::tick → Guide::tick_observed → Guide::advance → Guide::transaction`. The guide reads old arms, solves contact, constructs and checks M5, stages the two writes, commits both arms and updates its scalars. Only then does `Observation::read` use the new nut cursor and the period returned by that transaction. The three stations are owned values. The harness sends that observation's signed velocity directly to `O2Lane::tick → advance`. The current M5 sample is retained separately. No O2 value reaches a ring, contact solve, loss, branch or the M5 blocker. The offline three-depth render repeats the electrical calculation on independent lanes using that same observation.

| Disposition | Reproduced result |
|---|---|
| Prepared idle, reset and disposal | Four positive-zero histories. Legal cleared ticks emit positive zero with no physical commit or O2 call. Disposal invalidates once and resets O2. Repeated reset is exact. |
| Launch, active continuation and retune | Launch or replacement seeds the guide and resets all O2 histories before first use. Successful active ticks consume one same-tick observation. Continuous retune and compatible continuation keep the epoch and electrical history. |
| Physical lift and harness release interval | `k=0` keeps both successful guide and O2 processing live. The scheduled harness interval ends in explicit disposal. |
| Failed guide tick | At tick 700 the commit and O2 call counts stay at 653. The guide invalidates once, the physical fault counter advances once, and no sample or observation reaches O2. The harness clears the O2 lane. |
| Post-commit O2 fault | At tick 923 the counts advance once to 777, the physical fault count stays at one and invalidations advance by one. The committed physical sample is retained and delivered wet is absent. Subsequent legal cleared ticks advance neither counter. |
| Fresh epoch | At tick 1100 a new launch produces the next commit and electrical call. A separate fresh-lane comparison also matched M5, O1 and q bits after fault and relaunch. |
