# I1 source repeatability

## Registration before corrected wet measurement

The controlled class is the recorded guitar/pickup/string setting. Hold performer, nominal pluck point/force, pick, tuning, interface, gain, cable and channel fixed. For each two-class comparison take four independent attacks per setting, alternated A1 B1 … A4 B4. Use takes 1–2 for calibration and 3–4 withheld. No crops from one sustain count as repeats. DI engineering data retain the recording scalar. Detect the first |DI|>.01 FS, align this tick, reject clipping, require voiced 196 Hz within 15 cents and paired difference ≤10 cents on [80,230) ms (Hann H1 maximum, 0.025 Hz grid). Other pitches use the same relative search and identical prescribed carrier at that pitch. Take common recorded gain. A second perceptual set alone may use one documented scalar per complete clip.

Use symmetric Hann, mean removed, first twelve harmonic amplitudes, relative to their maximum, −50 dBr floor, then subtract the twelve-order mean. Distance is RMS component difference in dB. Freeze two signed cues before wet: sixth-order notch H6−(H5+H7)/2 and upper (H5–H10) vs lower (H1–H4) summed squared amplitude in dB. For either cue all referenced orders must exceed the analysis floor in both classes. Additional broad power summaries group H1–2, H3–4, H5–8, H9–12. Use the analysis reliability gate and report absent orders. DI on [80,230) ms, full wet on [2,3) s after support ends at .30 s. Same positive sign, 48 kHz, same pitch, V=.10 m/s, R=1.8, G=E=C=1. M5 and O2 use the same ticks. No per-note wet gain.

Use maximum same-class pair distance W and minimum cross-class distance B, separately on calibration and withheld sets. Require B−W≥1 dB for DI and ≥.5 dB for wet. Both withheld takes of each class must be closest to the correct arithmetic calibration centroid. At least one registered DI cue must have the same sign across all cross-class pairs with magnitude ≥2 dB. For every such cue require matching wet sign and magnitude ≥.5 dB on each withheld pair. Ties, absent required cues, reversed signs or insufficient separation hold the class claim. Report all pairs including failures. Neutral wet must be identical for both classes. These thresholds are proposed engineering decisions pending independent review and perception.

## Actual held full-wet results

The six G3 takes retained source gain for capture. Start at the first |DI|>.01 FS, accept injected 196 Hz/Q=.95 while actual A1 fast RMS supplies amplitude, close support at .30 s, leave zero DI thereafter. Each had 50 new-clock publications. Four-period frame smoothing used Movement=.5. The guide and all three returned O2 streams were identical between takes. G=E=1, σ positive, no direct or post processing. Matched-file scalars were applied **after** scoring. The H1 grid probe found 195.05–196.10 Hz. The measured-source-Hz DI projection and the measured-M5-Hz wet projection reduce leakage from small tuning differences.

Distances are dB RMS. Gain distances compare the twelve dB coordinates directly. Wet/DI distances use centered, floored log amplitudes. All fifteen pairings and the broad-band levels, per-order eligibility, signed contrasts and actual full-wet rows are retained in the results. Across distinct passages of one player, corrected wet distances were .144–1.692 dB; across the two players they were .110–1.707 dB. The distributions overlap. For 204 the coupled rule removed most of the between-take contrast. The P1/P2 settled ray fractions were 1/1, .625/1 and .125/.5 in passage order. For the synthetic contrast control, bright/dark held wet remained 2.406 dB apart. That pass establishes nontrivial transfer for that input.

At common gain the P1−P2 upper/lower cue was +7.41, +14.42 and +9.50 dB on DI, versus +1.70, +.33 and +.02 dB on corrected wet. The H6 notch contrasts were 0, −4.91 and −11.32 dB on DI, versus +.003, −.014 and +.021 dB on wet. The last changed sign and effectively vanished. These are diagnostics even when denominators are numerically measurable. Requiring *all* preregistered cue constituents to clear the noise/relative floor left no eligible cue shared by both sides of any of these three pairs. For P1/P2 passage 60, only two/four DI orders cleared ε on the measurement interval. The existing floor is defensible for rejecting unreliable evidence; this corpus does not validate .015, 3N₀, .45 or the four-period window as optimal for natural discrimination.

A separate historical replay set DI RMS to .08 FS on [60,220) ms and used the original fixed-pitch projection and .30 s capture. It reproduced 51 updates, between-player held distances 1.733/1.415/2.649 dB and within-player passage distances .548–2.618 dB. At 132 the original H6 notch contrast reversed from −4.648 to +.584 dB. The different DI gain convention changes which upper orders enter the feature. Neither this comparison nor the two FreePats velocity layers identifies a pickup effect.
