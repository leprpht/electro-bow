# From traveling wave to bowed electric guitar

The owner hears buzzing in the twelve isolated guide WAVs. At 440 Hz we reproduced the reported sawtooth envelope, its phase signature and the nearly full-amplitude first cycle. A1–A4's shipping fallback is a different signal path. This finding concerns the proposed supported-rate wet sound. We select a **spatially sensed, electrically loaded bowed string** with a bounded transfer from the player's DI. The owner explicitly selected the pickup-led reference in this increment. Neither the compact listening gate nor formal V22 has a human verdict.

## What the reference means

| Sound | Excitation, observation and likely audible distinction |
|---|---|
| Bowed electric guitar through magnetic pickups | Friction supplies repeated work. A coil responds to changing flux integrated along a short length of ferromagnetic string. Modal nodes and aperture cause partial cancellations, loading shapes the upper spectrum. A pickup does not directly measure bridge force. |
| Bowed acoustic instrument through air | A moving bridge excites a coupled body. Admittance changes reflection. Pressure per unit force also depends on direction, distance, room and radiation efficiency. A violin microphone recording can have peaks and zeros unrelated to pickup position. |
| Electronically sustained guitar | An electromagnetic driver can keep the actual string vibrating. Drive selectivity and pickup feedback matter. This offers no basis for inferring stick-slip, a specific proprietary circuit or a virtual player's force trajectory. |
| Selected musical effect | Physical bowed excitation heard through a virtual guitar pickup, with the incoming guitar's attack, continuous pitch, dynamics and captured spectral contrast. The direct contribution tends to zero at full wet. Borrow bowed articulation without imposing a violin-body transfer. |

## Observation derivation

Use SI units, positive transverse displacement in the bow direction, x in metres increasing from bridge to nut. For tension T [N], linear density μ [kg/m], μuₜₜ=Tuₓₓ gives c=√(T/μ) [m/s], Z=T/c [N·s/m]. Write u=l(t+x/c)+r(t−x/c). Then velocity v=l′+r′ [m/s], slope uₓ=(l′−r′)/c [dimensionless], and force **on the bridge** F=T uₓ=Z(l′−r′) [N]. The string sees the opposite reaction. At a fixed sinusoidal frequency ω≠0, U=V/(iω) [m]. At DC there is an unknown integration constant. A causal leaky integrator would add an arbitrary low-frequency pole and drift convention. Displacement is unnecessary for a first-order velocity pickup.

At x=0, l′ is incident, r′ reflected. At a rigid end r′=−l′, v_b=0 and F_b=2Zl′. At a moving end v_b=Y_b F_b [m/s], R_v=r′/l′=(ZY_b−1)/(ZY_b+1). Mechanical power into the body is F_b v_b=Z(l′²−r′²) [W]; sinusoidal mean is ½Re(F_b v_b*), using peak phasors. Measured pressure p_mic=H_pF F_b [Pa], with H_pF in Pa/N. Positive wave loss in a lumped reflection FIR establishes dissipation but does not specify the fraction or direction radiated. Even measured Y_b does not determine H_pF. For a fixed-end standing mode sin(kπs), rectangular sensitivity over width wL gives weight sin(kπs₀) sinc(kπw/2), with sinc(z)=sin(z)/z. The ideal two-ray factor on N has magnitude 2|sin(ω(d₂−d₁)/2)| in discrete radians/sample; at harmonic k this is 2|sin(kπs)|. A zero in the spatial sum changes both level and phase. The three-point Simpson rule is a bounded approximation of the spatial integral, with multiplier (2+cos(kπw/2))/3 on the ideal mode. At high spatial orders it misses continuous-aperture zeros. Bound use by the electrical bandwidth and verify at high notes and low sample rates. ## Why harmonic eight is already missing

With positive round-trip transfers H_N for nut delay and H_B for bridge delay plus loss, the signed recurrence gives B=j−H_N N and N=j−H_B B, hence (1−H_NH_B)B=(1−H_N)j. Net junction work is Fv, and the stored M4 ramp drives a Helmholtz-like orbit. H_N at harmonic k is near exp(+2πikβ). Its numerator nearly vanishes around k=1/.127=7.87. At 440 Hz measured B,N,p,j eighth-harmonic levels relative to their own fundamentals were −37.18, −36.30, −37.24 and −8.55 dB. The magnitudes of 1−H_N for orders 7–9 were −3.40, −19.61 and −1.29 dB. Complex residuals for the two identities, normalized by the B or p fundamental, were 2.03e−7 and 1.32e−8 RMS or less (orders 1–10). The additional pickup notches are an observation effect. Altering β to move the original notch would alter the qualified physics.

## Measurement method and comparisons

Regenerated 152 Rust trajectories. Read binary64 a,b,B,N,x,p,j,r,F,v,branch,M5 at their original indices. Use the last second of stationary data, Hann and 8× zero padding. Reference frequencies are the qualified pickup's interpolated peaks; measure complex Hann projections at k times that frequency, relative levels and φₖ−kφ₁. Centroid weights squared amplitude; residual is power outside the union of ±max(2 Hz,.001kf) harmonic bands. It includes leakage and folded or modulated energy. A period statistic warps consecutive measured slip-onset intervals to 128 samples and subtracts their mean cycle. Phase-origin quantization contributes to this error. No harmonicity metric scores realism. First 10 ms of low E spans less than one period.

Common gain throughout the following comparisons is .85 FS/(m/s), positive polarity, unit G, E, Out trim and no limiter. ## Attack finding and physical boundary

An amplitude envelope masks the launch step but cancels from a locally stationary harmonic ratio. In the 196 Hz controlled attack comparison both outputs use the same spatial/load path, 50 ms E schedule, target V=.10, R=1.8, lift at .7 s and 100 ms release. The expressive version launches at .06 and varies V and R over a 56 ms cubic ramp (U=.4). H8 in [30,55) ms changes from −16.05 to −18.45 dBr; after lift in [.71,.735) the two values converge to −23.67 and −23.82. This is a measurable articulation change; the listener still has to judge whether it resembles playing. Keep M4. If a level-matched, reference-guided listener repeatedly rejects the entry despite these trajectories, document the timestamp and a missing measured transient (e.g. preperiodic slip formation). Seek separate permission for a from-rest launch investigation and requalification of startup, force, pitch and energy before touching feedback.
