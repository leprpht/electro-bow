# Guitar identity through full-wet sustain

At full Character and E=1 the signed-off A4 mix removes the guitar waveform. A different DI can have the same continuous pitch, captured level/polarity, virtual force, speed and opening event. With identical histories of those quantities the deterministic M5 samples are identical. This is observable on independent Rust replays below. A preserved bend is evidence of performance tracking. It does not establish that a listener can tell the sources apart during a held note.

## What can survive a pluck

| Feature | When it is informative | Ambiguity or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Relative levels of supported partials | After the broadband pick onset, over several periods, at matching pitch and level. Low/middle harmonic groups often distinguish a string, picking position or pickup. | Pluck position introduces its own nodes, pick material and direction change tilt, decay rates differ by partial. Magnet location, playing position, electronics and string cannot be factored uniquely from one channel. |
| Narrow notch between supported neighbors | With persistent evidence above the local noise floor. Position weighting has a physical sinusoidal modal dependence. Two wired coils can add further cancellation. | A true zero and an inaudibly weak/noisy partial are indistinguishable. Fixed nominal pickup geometry can have its own coincident null. An output-only gain cannot restore an absent guide component. |
| Smooth tilt, relative high-partial strength | Useful even if exact harmonic phases or pickup model are unknown. Absolute pickup resonances occur at particular Hz; modal nodes move with string/fret and geometry. | A scalar brightness omits shape. Broad averaging can erase opposite changes in adjacent orders. A tone control, amp chain or a harder stroke can impersonate a different instrument. |
| Timing, polarity, pitch motion, level | Causally observable from original audio subject to existing confirmation. Useful for direct attack and virtual control. | Onset is a pluck. It does not specify bow pressure or acceleration. A pitch error sends a harmonic measurement to the wrong partial. At very weak levels or an ambiguous chord, freeze the last supported evidence. |

Smith's STFT envelope cross-synthesis separates slowly varying spectral shape from excitation. A broadband STFT/cepstral envelope smooths across harmonic holes according to its quefrency cutoff. At high f₀ there are few samples of an underlying envelope; a very fine envelope tracks individual pluck peaks, and division by a spectral valley amplifies noise. Whole-frame resynthesis would introduce a different wet time/phase boundary. We use past-window projections for measurement only. [Smith, *Spectral Audio Signal Processing*, STFT Applications](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/sasp/Applications_STFT.html)

Csound 6.18.1 (tag object `a1580f9cdf331c35dceb486f4231871ce0b00266`), `util/hetro.c::hetdyn`, multiplies a recorded sample sequence by sine and cosine at an analysis harmonic, then averages/low-passes the quadratures. The resulting magnitude and phase derivative describe amplitude and instantaneous frequency. It is an offline utility with an assumed fundamental, not a production detector. In `Opcodes/pvsbasic.c::pvsfilter` the streaming amplitude/frequency frame path computes output amplitude = input amplitude × gain × [(1−depth)+depth·filter amplitude] and preserves the input frequency bin field. This illustrates carrier-preserving magnitude control. Its scale, window and phase-vocoder latency would need a separate contract. We derive the measurements independently, use no Csound code, dependencies or reconstructed oscillator bank. Both files carry LGPL-2.1-or-later notices. [Heterodyne source](https://github.com/csound/csound/blob/6.18.1/util/hetro.c#L386-L456) · [Streaming filter source](https://github.com/csound/csound/blob/6.18.1/Opcodes/pvsbasic.c#L1532-L1585)

For an actual causal streaming front end at the same revision, Csound’s `OOps/pvsanal.c::anal_tick` accumulates a hop, then `generate_frame` windows its circular history and calculates a real FFT. At bin j it extracts magnitude and a frequency offset from the wrapped interframe phase increment, scaled by the frame rate. Window-center time precedes publication. This is an implementation precedent for keeping the observation timestamp separate from its availability. Our estimator samples specific kf values on a trailing window and leaves the guide phase in the carrier. The source carries LGPL-2.1-or-later. [Streaming analysis source](https://github.com/csound/csound/blob/6.18.1/OOps/pvsanal.c#L271-L440)

Horton & Moore's measured flux response and Paiva et al.'s pickup position/width/loading models explain why the DI itself contains partial relationships worth retaining. None supplies the inverse problem solution for an unknown single pickup signal. Published equations are used as research references, with no reproduction of tables, figures or third-party DSP. [Horton & Moore (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.2990663) · [Paiva, Pakarinen & Välimäki (2012)](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=16551)

## Selected transfer

Use the first twelve **observable** harmonic orders at the confirmed pitch, with a trailing four-period Hann window. On a 5 ms hop, center log(k·amplitude) across supported harmonics, shrink each contrast by reliability and .45, bound it to ±5 dB and smooth it. A missing/weak partial gets no compensatory boost. Apply the gains to the guide's loaded pickup through pairs of contractive complex poles driven by the real carrier, leaving its ungained direct component in place. These filters are zero-input stable and silent on reset; they cannot sustain a note on their own. Recompute the centers during retune. The held state is twelve bounded numbers, plus the ordinary filter histories. There is no continued DI requirement and no synthesis of new partials. The normative proposal specifies the exact projection, decimator, per-order confidence, smoothing, gain interpolation in time/frequency, numerical guards and overlap. [Implementation equations](../architecture/ELECTROBOW_ACOUSTIC_OUTPUT_SPECIFICATION.md#i1-causal-source-feature-and-confidence)

A four-band ablation exposed a limitation worth keeping in the tests. Map the same weighted log contrasts to linearly interpolated log-frequency knots 120/450/1400/3500 Hz, add unit neutral prior per band, clip at ±4 dB, apply through the existing complementary 200/800/3000 Hz filters. On the three labeled pairs it transmitted only .18–.35 dB. The final partial-wise mechanism preserves more of the measured distinctions. Both calculations operate on the same Rust carrier offline. Only the partial-wise mechanism is proposed for implementation.

## Measured identifiability

Input selection is independent of the virtual bow. For each real DI use the first |x|>.01 FS in the extracted clip as onset. For this experiment only, match inputs to .08 FS RMS in [.06,.22) seconds after onset. Inject the same nominal Hz for both takes, V=.10, R=1.8, G=1, σ=+1. The nominal pitch assumption can create small detuning error; it is deliberately identical across a pair. At the recorded rate, measure past-window features after 25 ms, using fixed N₀=.00032 and Movement=.5. Capture snapshots at .16, .22 and .30 s. Render a common carrier at 48 kHz, freeze each .30 s vector and measure the sustained half-second with direct gain zero. This isolates captured state from virtual controls. The steady-state offline calculation applies the snapshot as an injected initial condition; the Rust per-hop scheduler, 15 ms sample slew and live loss-of-support transitions require their own validation. The M5 negative control runs the renderer twice on the same controls and compares actual output arrays byte-for-byte. [Executable experiment](../../tools/validation/acoustic_identity.py)

For each stem project twelve partials, express log magnitudes relative to the greatest partial, floor at −50 dBr for measurement only, subtract their mean and use RMS distance in dB. Correlation compares the order-by-order input difference with the wet difference. “Within” is the same wet metric when **only** acquisition duration changes .16→.30 s on each take. It does not measure repeat performances.

The Guitar-TECHS recordings have known guitars, pickup positions and separate players. Compare the three held G3 extracts within each player: pairwise distances .55/2.05/1.89 dB (P1) and 1.51/2.62/1.37 dB (P2). These overlap the between-player distances. The signal encodes source-dependent differences; the current corpus **does not establish reliable pickup classification across performances**. String, fret, dynamics and player are confounded. It would be inappropriate to set a passing identity threshold just below the weakest between-player result.

For a local notch at order k use Dₖ=Lₖ−(Lₖ₋₁+Lₖ₊₁)/2 in dB. The difference of D₆ between the Q high-E pair was +3.10 dB direct, +1.79 dB wet. For the third labeled G3 pair it was −11.16/−2.10 dB; the second pair gave −4.65/+0.58 dB. Notch polarity is not uniformly preserved. On controlled 1/k harmonics, changing the sixth's amplitude factor from 1 to .35 to 0 produced sixth-order targets +.0004, −3.64 and 0 dB: an observable dip survives, an unsupported null gets neutral gain. Silence and an input below the floor yielded no update. These are intentional limits on deep-notch transfer. [Complete gain vectors and results](../../build/stage-04/identity.json)

With the P1 G3 feature frozen, replaying the actual moving N wave through all twelve time-varying correction filters gave peaks .4428 FS (bend) and .4417 FS (vibrato), correction RMS .00774 FS in each case. On the fixed guide windows the emitted pitch errors were 2.17 and 2.47 cents RMS, at 0 ms integer lag. This tests a frozen source feature while the carrier follows prescribed pitch. Release scheduling and confidence-loss detection remain combined-controller tests.

## Evidence still needed

The FreePats recorded soft/higher-velocity E2 layers add a control on dynamics. Both source files peak near full scale. On their native-gain excerpts, the maximum A1 fast level in the first 50 ms after A crosses .002 FS is .597 FS for the soft sample and .417 FS for the higher layer. Both saturate the proposed U and G capture. Peak level alone would reverse their ordering here. Their attack and spectral evolution are usable as labeled fixtures; a known common preamp gain remains necessary to qualify the amplitude-to-bow mapping. [Versioned sample bank and mappings](https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-direct/tree/4c1d54601836c3e1a262b0bb0f3dea0c0a881ad3)

Freeze one matched string, fret, pick position and gain with two identifiable pickups, ideally simultaneously recorded from separate outputs or reamped from a repeatable mechanical pluck. Two takes per pickup at minimum; include one soft and one hard gesture separately rather than inferring technique labels from peak amplitude. Keep an untouched repeat for blind matching. Check identity across timbral differences, warm/bright reversal, pitch level, unsupported decay and an actual player's onset. The labeled two-player and same-pitch string fixtures already permit a compact first gate without requiring the owner to tune any parameter. Approve a repeatability-based criterion before exposing listening labels. [Corpus, gates and blind protocol](../implementation/ACOUSTIC_VALIDATION_PLAN.md)
