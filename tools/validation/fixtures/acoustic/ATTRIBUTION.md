# Acoustic research excerpts

**techs-*.wav** — Hegel Pedroza, Wallace Abreu, Ryan M. Corey and Iran R. Roman, *Guitar-TECHS*, ICASSP 2025, Zenodo record 14963133. Used under CC BY 4.0. The six numbered files are one-second extracts from the original DI of the Ibanez PF300 bridge pickup (player 1) and EVH Wolfang neck pickup (player 2, dataset spelling). The other three extracts are two seconds each of player 1's named techniques. Guitar and performer differ between the two players. Changes made here are excerpting, selecting the first channel and encoding mono IEEE float WAV at the source rate, unity gain. [Authors and setup](https://guitar-techs.github.io/) · [Dataset and archives](https://zenodo.org/records/14963133) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

For P=1 or 2 use `P{P}_singlenotes.zip`, member `P{P}_singlenotes/audio/directinput/directinput_allsinglenotes.wav`. For filename time T in seconds extract sample indices [(T−.2)fs,(T+.8)fs). For the named techniques use `P1_techniques.zip`, member `P1_techniques/audio/directinput/directinput_{Vibrato,PalmMute,Bendings}.wav`, [0,2fs). Integer PCM is normalized by its signed full scale. The files preserve source level differences.

**vaisvil-*.wav** — Vaisvil, *bowed guitar-4.mp3*, Freesound sound 103565, used under CC BY 4.0. A viola bow on a Stratocaster copy with Lace pickups. Excerpted from the HQ preview, decoded using Apple AudioToolbox, left channel, unity gain, no EQ or normalization. The filename gives the start second, the duration is one second at 44100 Hz. The full preview's SHA-256 is 72b4f95f8210816112509d4888650e1fc8247d07c2e7fb4789e760dad0aad608. Codec delay or decoder implementations can differ; the included WAVs are the scoring reference. [Original and author](https://freesound.org/people/vaisvil/sounds/103565/) · [HQ preview](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/103/103565_1735967-hq.mp3) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**fsbs-*.wav** — FreePats, *Electric Guitar FSBS (direct)*, version 2022-09-11, revision `4c1d54601836c3e1a262b0bb0f3dea0c0a881ad3`, CC0 1.0. Basic Fender electric guitar, bridge pickup, dedicated interface input. These are the first second of native E2 string-1 take 01 for the SFZ soft layer (MIDI velocity ≤92 in the source bank) and high-velocity layer (≥93). Those labels identify recorded samples; ElectroBow uses only their audio. Mono, original 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM converted to float32 at unity gain. Upstream full-WAV SHA-256 is 5975ccbb88f76d8b165de11d0022c4344d05f03f5d6b994aafdf60cf9245d43c (soft) and be2f50fb2c4cee7c76a91dbfa979de1ff1cd28de25d0900d83d52aed746b857a (higher layer). [Original bank, mapping, samples and license](https://github.com/freepats/electric-guitar-FSBS-direct/tree/4c1d54601836c3e1a262b0bb0f3dea0c0a881ad3) · [CC0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Source authors do not endorse this effect. Preserve these credits with copies and listening excerpts derived from the recordings. Full fixture hashes, gain, pitch and use are in the [validation plan](../../../../docs/implementation/ACOUSTIC_VALIDATION_PLAN.md).

Stage 06 uses a longer low-E phrase from the same Vaisvil preview. `vaisvil-low-e-stroke.wav` takes left-channel samples [975712,1052888) after Apple AudioToolbox float32 decode at 44100 Hz (22.125 to 23.875 seconds, rounded to nearest sample). The level falls, rises into a sustained pitch and falls again during continuous playing. Bow contact times and release into free decay are unannotated. Same CC BY 4.0 credit and source above. The file preserves recording gain.
